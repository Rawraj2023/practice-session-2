Conjecture

Based on computational evidence, the chromatic number of the natural central digraph G(ℤk) satisfies:

χ(G(ℤₖ)) = 4 if k = 3 χ(G(ℤₖ)) = 5 if k ≥ 4 (conjectured)

Equivalently: χ = k+1 at k=3; χ stabilizes at 5 for all k ≥ 4.

Universal properties (all k ≥ 3)

  • ω = 3: the graph always contains triangles but no K₄ (independent of k)
  • Connected: the underlying undirected graph is always connected
  • Girth 3: odd cycles present; prevents 2-coloring
  • Self-loops at (p, 0) for p ∈ ℤ/kℤ (removed for undirected graph)
  • Degree: vertices have degree k−1 or k (near-regular)
  • n/α ≈ 4: fractional chromatic number bounds χ ≥ 4 for k ≥ 4
